NPU sysMon comparison: five captures of NCM

Hexagon SDK sysMon recorded the NPU for 10 seconds in five situations: an idle board, NCM running on the CPU, and three versions of NCM running on the NPU. This page puts the parser's numbers and graphs for all five side by side.

Tool: sysMonAppLE + sysmon_parser (Hexagon SDK 5.5.7)Runtime: QAIRT 2.50, 16-bit mixed buildCaptured 2026-10-24, NPU runs pinned to cores 4–7

Overall summary

Rows marked "parser" are copied from the parser's dcvs_metric.txt. "Time-averaged" rows are computed here from the parser's samples, each weighted by the time it covers (about 1 ms while the NPU is busy, about 200 ms while it is idle). Utilisation is the effective work rate divided by the clock. "Busy share" is a separate measurement: NPU compute per story from QNN profiling, divided by the normal time per story.

Read this before quoting the numbers

  • sysMon slows what it measures. While recording, the first version ran at about 140 ms per story (126 normally) and shared buffers at 139 (105). For how busy the NPU really is, use the QNN busy share (46%, 55%, 91%).
  • sysMon stalled the batched run. Its NPU calls came every 44 ms (median) for the first 3 s and slowed a little after; from 3.85 s on there were only 10 calls, with gaps of 0.55, 0.76, 1.1, 1.3 and 1.4 s. The whole run still averaged 105 ms per story, so it must have sped up again after sysMon stopped. Its 30% utilisation and ~208 ms per story therefore reflect sysMon, not the setup; per-call cycles and DDR traffic per call are not affected by the gaps.
  • Busy share carries about ±15%. NPU compute per story was profiled with qnn-net-run in one session (4.76 ms per generator call at batch 1). The first version uses that batch-1 figure: same model, but its decoder returned logits (0.85 ms, measured in an earlier session). That earlier session measured 4.15 ms per generator call, so absolute values move between sessions; the ranking does not.
  • Different counters, different figures. For the first version the parser gives AXI read as 2,308 MB/s in its overall summary (the 128-byte and 256-byte counters added: 111 + 2,197), 111 MB/s in its processor table (128-byte, averaged over active samples), 99 MB/s in dcvs_metric.txt, while the 128-byte samples average 61 MB/s over time. It also reports two active times (6.08 s in dcvs_metric.txt, 5.34 s in Summary.csv) and two average clocks for the batched run (1,246 MHz from its NPA votes, 1,148 MHz time-weighted). Compare captures within one row, not across rows.
  • HMX is left out. The parser prints HMX figures, but this v68 NPU has no HMX unit.
  • One capture each. The idle, first-version, shared-buffer and batched captures were taken in one session, starting below 60 °C; the CPU capture after a reboot.
  • The DSP hung two minutes after the batched capture: that program took 11 s to close its NPU session (normally 3–7 ms), then the kernel logged glink-edge: intent request timed out every 10 s until a reboot. With the stall above, this points at sysMon combined with long batched NPU calls. Plain runs of the same setup closed normally and did not hang; do not record the multi-lane setup with sysMon.
